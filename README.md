## ∈ About me

I'm a high school student with a strong interest in mathematics, computer science, and economics, building toward a career in quantitative finance.

I enjoy problems that hide their structure: finding a useful representation, breaking a problem into cases, and discovering why an unexpected solution works. Probability and combinatorics are particular interests of mine.

I value creative thinking, clear explanations, and persistence when an approach fails. I want to understand the reasoning behind a method well enough to reconstruct it and apply it in a different context.

## ∑ Mathematical interests

- Probability, conditional reasoning, and Bayesian thinking.
- Combinatorics, counting techniques, and constrained arrangements.
- Expected value and decision-making under uncertainty.
- Random processes, pattern races, and state-based models.
- Mathematical modeling and computational simulation.

## ∩ Python & projects

I use Python in my projects and am developing my ability to translate mathematical reasoning into clear, testable code.

My interests include using computation to explore probability, test conjectures, simulate random processes, and compare theoretical predictions with experimental results.

I'm particularly interested in projects connecting mathematics, data, and quantitative finance.

## ∆ Current direction

- Deepening my foundations in probability and mathematical reasoning.
- Strengthening Python, algorithms, and computational problem-solving.
- Learning more about statistics, optimization, and quantitative research.
- Working toward studying Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of São Paulo.

## ∴ How I approach problems

**Understand the rules → build a model → test small cases → justify the result → explore variations.**

A useful simulation can test an idea. A mathematical argument explains why it works.
